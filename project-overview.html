Clock-In_V2

"JobSim" — A Multiplayer Educational Job Simulation Game

Unity 3D C# Photon PUN 2 Multiplayer Educational

🎯 Core Concept

An open-world job simulation game where players experience different real-world careers through interactive mini-games. The design philosophy is educational authenticity — every task is grounded in reality and explains why it's done that way (e.g., why electrical sockets are mounted at a specific height for accessibility).

💼 Job Categories

💻 Tech & IT / Game Dev

Networking education — TCP/IP, routing, protocols, packet delivery gameplay.

🍳 Food & Service

Kitchen/chef job — order taking, grilling, restocking.

🚗 Transportation & Repair

Auto shop — oil changes, tire pressure, part identification and matching.

🔨 Construction & Maintenance

Ceiling tiles, painting, wood assembly.

🌳 Outdoor & Utility Work

Landscaping and general repairs.
