01 · The Concept

What is Clock-In?

Clock-In drops players into an open world where different careers are scattered around town — and lets them try the job.

Walk into a career world, take on a short, hands-on scenario, and complete skill-based tasks that capture what that job is actually like. Every task has a reason behind it, so players leave knowing a little about a real profession and the skills it takes.

At a glance

  • Open-world career tour
  • Hands-on mini-worlds per job
  • Multiplayer — play together
  • Learn by doing, not reading

02 · How It Works

The Loop

1

Enter a world

Pick a career and drop into its mini-world from the shared town.

2

Take the scenario

A short setup frames the job — what's happening and what needs doing.

3

Do the tasks

Complete skill-based challenges unique to that profession.

4

Learn the "why"

Each task explains why it's done that way — the real-world reason behind it.

5

Try the next job

Head back out and clock in somewhere new.

04 · Spotlight

Inside the Networking World

1

Pick up a packet

Data packets appear in the level — grab one to carry it.

2

Cross the live grid

The floor is tiles that fade in and out on timers. Time your route.

3

Deliver to the goal

Reach the destination — the packet lands and the delivery counts.

What it teaches

  • Packets — data travels in pieces
  • Routing — finding a path to the destination
  • Timing — a network changes moment to moment
  • Delivery — getting data where it belongs

06 · Multiplayer

Playing Together

Clock-In is multiplayer to its core, built on Photon PUN 2. Players connect to a shared server, join a room, and drop into the same world together — movement synced in real time.

CONNECT
Join the Photon server & lobby
›
ROOM
Host creates or joins a room
›
LAUNCH
Host loads a career world for everyone
›
SPAWN
Each player spawns into the world
›
PLAY
Movement syncs across the room

Under the hood

A persistent room manager keeps players in sync across scene loads, and a networked player manager spawns the right character for each world. Position and rotation sync on change; critical data syncs reliably.
