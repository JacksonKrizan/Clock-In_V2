Firefighter World - Mini Games

The things a firefighter actually does, turned into mini games you play in first person. Listed easy to hard. Nothing here is built yet - these are the ideas to start from.

1. Put Out the Fire

Status: to do

What it teaches: the basics of fighting a fire - act fast and aim at the base of the flames, not the top.

How you play it: fires break out around the room as glowing flame objects. You grab an extinguisher (or hose nozzle), look at a fire, and hold the button to spray it. Aim low at the base and it shrinks and goes out. Leave it too long and it grows and spreads to the next spot. Put them all out to score. This is the firefighter version of Packet Delivery - the simple first game.

2. Right Extinguisher

Status: to do

What it teaches: not every fire is put out the same way. A grease fire, an electrical fire, and a wood fire each need a different extinguisher, and using the wrong one can make it worse.

How you play it: a fire flares up with a symbol over it (a grease pan, sparking wires, or burning wood). A rack on the wall has a few labeled extinguishers. You read the fire, grab the matching extinguisher, and spray it. Right match puts it out and scores. Wrong match flares the fire up and costs you.

3. Check the Door

Status: to do

What it teaches: always feel a door for heat before opening it. Opening a hot door can let the fire blow out at you.

How you play it: you move through a building with several closed doors. Look at a door to check it - a cool door turns green and is safe to open and walk through, a hot one turns red and you have to leave it shut and find another way. Pick the safe path through the building. Open a hot door and you take a hit and get sent back.

4. Search and Rescue

Status: to do

What it teaches: get people out of a burning building, and stay low because smoke rises and the safe air is near the floor.

How you play it: rooms are filling with smoke near the ceiling, so you crouch to move safely (standing in the smoke drains a health/air bar). You search the rooms, find trapped people, pick them up and carry them to the exit before the timer runs out. Each person you get out scores. More people and thicker smoke on harder rounds.

5. Hook Up the Hydrant

Status: to do

What it teaches: the water has to get from the supply to the fire. You lay out hose and connect it up, and hose only reaches so far.

How you play it: the fire is across the lot from the hydrant or truck. You connect hose sections end to end to build a line from the water to the fire. Each section only reaches so far, so you route it around obstacles using the fewest sections. Once the line connects, the water flows and you put the fire out. This is the fire version of Build the Route.

6. Get Everyone Out

Status: to do

What it teaches: evacuation - lead people along the safe escape route and keep them away from doors and halls that are blocked by fire.

How you play it: a group of people needs to reach the exit, but some hallways are blocked by flames. You lead the way and they follow. Pick the route that avoids the fire and get the whole group to the exit. Lose people to a blocked path and your score drops. Mixes the door checking and rescue ideas into one bigger round.

Plan: start with Put Out the Fire so spraying and flames work, then Right Extinguisher reuses that. Check the Door and Search and Rescue build the "move through a burning building" feel. Hook Up the Hydrant and Get Everyone Out are the bigger ones for later.

Note for setup: the player spawner (PlayerManager.cs) currently spawns the fire player for a scene named "Chef", but the fire scene is "Firemap". That mapping will need a "Firemap" case before this world loads the right player.
