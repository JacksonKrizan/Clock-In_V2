01 · Introduction

What is Clock-In?

Clock-In is a 3D multiplayer game that lets players try on real-world careers — without leaving the classroom.

Each job is a hands-on mini-game built around what that career actually involves: routing data on a network, repairing a car, working a kitchen, or responding to a fire. Players jump into a shared world, take on a role, and learn by doing.

Our goal: make career exploration fun, social, and interactive for students deciding what they want to be.

04 · Gameplay Spotlight

Inside the Networking Mini-Game

Our deepest job — it turns abstract networking ideas into something you can run across.

1
PICK UP THE PACKET
Cyan-glowing packets appear in the level — grab one to carry it.
2
CROSS THE LIVE GRID
The floor is tiles that fade in and out on 3–10s cycles. Time your route.
3
DELIVER TO THE GOAL
Reach the goal zone — the packet turns green and you score points.
4
SYNCED FOR EVERYONE
Packet state syncs over Photon, so every player sees the same delivery live.

WHAT IT TEACHES

Packets — data travels in pieces

Routing — finding a path to the destination

Timing & latency — networks change moment to moment

Synchronization — keeping every node in agreement

Built from real scripts:

Packet · PacketGoal · Tiles · ScoreManager

05 · Multiplayer

Playing Together with Photon PUN 2

Clock-In is multiplayer to its core. Players connect to a shared lobby, join a room, and drop into the same job together — in real time.

CONNECT

Players join the Photon master server and lobby

›
ROOM

Host creates or joins a room; everyone sees the player list

›
LAUNCH

Host picks a job and loads the level for the whole room

SPAWN

Each player spawns the right character for that scene

›
PLAY

Movement & scores sync live across all players

Under the hood: a persistent RoomManager keeps players in sync across scene loads, a networked PlayerManager spawns the correct character per job, and host-controlled level loading keeps the whole room on the same map.

06 · Live Scoreboard

Competing in Real Time

Every delivery, repair, and task earns points. A live scoreboard shows where everyone stands — instantly, for all players in the room.

Backend-agnostic core
A single ScoreManager owns the score and fires events — gameplay never talks to the network directly.
Synced over Photon
ScoreNetworkSync pushes each score into Photon properties, so all players see updates live.
Toggle anytime
Pop the full scoreboard, sorted highest-first, mid-match — plus a clean always-on HUD.

07 · Accounts & Leaderboard

Cloud Progression with Firebase

New for Nationals: players can create an account, sign in, and have their best scores saved to a global leaderboard in the cloud.

ACCOUNTS

  • Sign up with email & display name
  • Secure sign-in via Firebase Auth
  • Continue as Guest — no account needed
  • Session persists across scenes
  • One-tap sign-out

GLOBAL LEADERBOARD

  • Personal-best scores saved to cloud
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Ranked top-N global standings
  • Guests stay private (never written)
  • Tracks progress across sessions

WHY IT MATTERS

  • Turns one match into a season
  • Rewards coming back & improving
  • Casual OR committed — your choice
  • Shows real backend engineering
  • Scales to any number of players

Our workflow

We work in GitHub branches and pull requests — each feature is built in isolation, reviewed, then merged. Tickets track every task. Unity lets us iterate on gameplay fast, while clean code organization (separate folders per job, shared core systems) keeps a four-job game manageable for a three-person team.
